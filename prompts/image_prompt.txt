Draw the thing itself, not a metaphor for it. If the topic is an array, draw
indexed cells. If it is a request, draw the request and the response. A glowing
abstract network does not teach anyone anything.

Composition:
- One idea only. A single diagram, centred, with room around it.
- 1:1 square.
- Dark background, one accent colour, high contrast.
- Flat vector style: clean strokes, solid fills, no gradients on the shapes.
- Labels only where they carry meaning — a few words at most, never a sentence
  and never a paragraph.
- Readable as a thumbnail on a phone.

Avoid:
- Stock-photo imagery, people at laptops, handshakes, glowing brains.
- Floating abstract particles, generic "tech" swooshes, circuit-board texture.
- Invented numbers, fake charts, or axis labels that mean nothing.
- Company logos or any trademarked mark.
- Lens flare, 3D bevels, drop shadows, neon glow.

The test: someone who has not read the post should still learn something true
about {{TOPIC}} from the picture alone.
